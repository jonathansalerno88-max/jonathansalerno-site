I am a fifth-year medical student in Florence, working in two areas.

The first is secondary prevention. The category gathers conditions that are underneath deeply different, and that uniformity propagates: states that would call for different decisions end up with the same diagnosis and the same treatment.

The second is pharmacokinetics — in vitro–in vivo extrapolation and drug–drug interaction. I am working on whether Ki-to-AUCR prediction can be improved from classical kinetic data alone, read through a different lens.

The two are nearer than they look: in both, one label or one number stands in for states that do not behave alike.

Published

Beyond Km and Vmax: a geometric framework for characterising enzyme modulation and validating kinetic data

2026

Drug Metabolism and Pharmacokinetics

Modulation of an enzyme is usually reported as a change in two parameters read separately. Here the modulated state is normalised against its own native control and read as a single point on a plane, where position carries the direction of the effect, its size, and an internal consistency check on the data behind it.

Drafts, not yet submitted.

  • One label, two states: what removing the cause reveals in secondary prevention Secondary prevention files under one label findings of two kinds: a disposition that reverts once its cause is removed, and a disease process already under way. Removing the cause is what tells them apart. Treated alike, the first is over-treated and the second under-treated, at the same time.
  • When to test, when to treat, when to ask: a threshold model for preclinical findings The quantitative companion to the paper above. It adds the axis of damage and turns the distinction into thresholds for acting on a finding before it has caused harm.
  • Who posits the ends of medicine? Quaternary prevention and the missing criterion of excess Quaternary prevention asks medicine to stop at what is too much, but excess can only be measured against an end. Medicine cannot do without ends, even where the data it works on can; the end of a treatment is set by doctor and patient together.
  • The incommunicability of being: from Gorgias to Mary’s room to the advance directive Who a person is and what a person is like are different questions: the first settles identity, the second capacity. Followed through an advance directive written before dementia, a hypothetical quasi-clone, and a model trained on someone who has died.

Software

Diario Salute

Structured tracking of physiological measurements, built so that what a patient records between visits arrives at their doctor in a form that can actually be read.

Calculators

Alcohol, body mass index, pack-years and weekly physical activity. Each measure is shown with the definition it depends on, the threshold that follows from it, and the source. In six languages.

Catalytic Plane Reader

Put in a native and a modulated Km–Vmax pair and see the same displacement drawn four ways at once: saturation curve, Lineweaver–Burk, the catalytic plane, and the circle-and-triangle geometry.

Study support

Light, the eye and lenses

Light, the eye and lenses, worked through with interactive figures: lumens, candelas, lux and nits with bike lights, projectors and screens; luminance and glare, how the retina is damaged, refractive errors and accommodation, the iris muscles and the drugs that act on them, pupil size and aberrations, spectacle lenses, parallax, thin lenses, real and virtual images. Also in Italian.

Microbiology

Microbiology, one block at a time, with interactive figures. It starts with the bacterial cell: Gram-positives, Gram-negatives and mycobacteria side by side, from wall and membrane to flagella and pili, with Gram and Ziehl–Neelsen stains and where antibiotics act. Then two guided clinical cases: meningococcal meningitis and tuberculosis. Also in Italian.

Clinical cases

Interactive clinical cases: choose the treatment and watch what happens to the patient. The first is heart failure, with oedema, ascites, diuretics, the RAAS and the four pillars, and its look-alikes: cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome, cor pulmonale and drug-induced oedema. Also in Italian.

Transporters

The three routes across a membrane, animated: diffusion, channels and carriers, passive or active; then the transporters of the nephron, segment by segment. Also in Italian.

Portal circulations

Two capillary beds in series: liver, pituitary and kidney side by side; then the kidney in detail, with its two arterioles, the RAAS, the sympathetic system and diuretics. Also in Italian.

Fundamental formulas

The formulas of physiology read one variable at a time, with units and dimensional analysis, starting with the Starling equation. Also in Italian.
