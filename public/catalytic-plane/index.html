Kinetic type

What the scheme reads. Polarity comes from the hypotenuse — its tilt is the clearance ratio, so a tilt past 45° is activation. The circle measures how far the state has moved, never in which direction. The triangle flags a pair whose product has drifted, which is where a transcription or unit error usually shows up.

What it does not do. Ki and Ki′ are inverted under one binding site and rapid equilibrium; neither holds for time-dependent inactivation. A ratio pair carries no information about the quality of the fit it came from, so a wide confidence interval on Vmax passes through this tool silently.
