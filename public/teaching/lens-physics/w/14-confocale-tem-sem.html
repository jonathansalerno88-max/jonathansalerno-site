Three microscopes in diagram form. Confocal: a laser lights up one point of the sample and a pinhole in front of the detector blocks the light coming from above and below the focal plane; you choose an open or closed pinhole and the depth of the plane. TEM: a beam of electrons passes through an ultra-thin section, magnetic lenses form the image on a screen; you choose thickness, heavy-metal staining and focus. SEM: a beam of electrons scans the surface line by line and a detector collects the secondary electrons; you choose whether to coat the sample with metal and start the scan.
