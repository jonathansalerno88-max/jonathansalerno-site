Diagram of a compound optical microscope, from bottom to top: light source, condenser, slide with the letter e, objective, intermediate image, eyepiece, eye. You choose whether the condenser, the objective and the eyepiece are converging or diverging lenses and you turn the focus knob; on the right you see what appears in the eyepiece. Only with three converging lenses and the right focus does the letter appear large, sharp, bright and upside down.
