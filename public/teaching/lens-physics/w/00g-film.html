A film, shot at 24 fps like almost all cinema, on a 120 Hz TV. Here lag does not matter: nobody presses buttons.

Simplified model. Persistence P: how long each image stands still; blur = speed × P, with an object crossing 1,920 px in 2 s (960 px/s). Standard interpolation, outside game mode, delays the picture by tens or hundreds of ms; with films the TV delays the sound too to keep them together. The little square moves only with the images the TV shows, but your screen shows at most as many as it can (usually 60 per second): enough to see the difference between 24 and 120.
