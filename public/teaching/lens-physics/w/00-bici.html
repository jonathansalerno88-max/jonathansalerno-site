Simplified model: uniform beam inside a cone, seen from above; real headlights have a centre brighter than the average. Braking 5 m/s² dry and 3 m/s² wet; 1 s reaction. Target at the stopping distance: 10 lux (lx = lm/m²) with street lights, 30 lux on a dark road. Candelas: cd = lm/sr. Cost: a rough estimate, about €15 + €4 per 100 lumens, plus a few euros for narrow-beam optics; real prices vary a lot with brand and battery.
