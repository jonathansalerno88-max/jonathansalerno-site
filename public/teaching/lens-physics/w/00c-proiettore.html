Simplified model. Contrast = luminance of white / luminance of black (nits / nits). Projector on a matte screen: the screen sends back the projector's light and the room light with the same reflectance (white ρ 0.9; ALR 0.8 for the projector and 0.15 for the room), native contrast 1,000:1. LED TV: 400 nits (cd/m²), reflects 1.5% of the light, native contrast 3,000:1; OLED TV: 250 nits, reflects 1%, near-perfect black. Goals from the AVIXA/ANSI image contrast standard: 15:1 for basic decision making (slides), 80:1 for video.
