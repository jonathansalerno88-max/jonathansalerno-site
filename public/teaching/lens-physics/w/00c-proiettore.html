Simplified model. Matte screen: L = ρ · E / π. White screen ρ 0.9, light grey 0.5, ALR 0.8 for projector light and 0.15 for room light. Projector: native contrast 1,000:1. LED TV: 400 nits (cd/m²), reflects 1.5% of the light; OLED TV: 250 nits, reflects 1%. ‘Looks good’: contrast at least 10:1 and white at least 3 times the luminance of the wall (minimum 48 nits, the cinema standard). Made-up prices, only for comparison: projector €200 + €0.30 per lumen; white screen €80 + €1.5 per inch, grey €120 + €2, ALR €300 + €12; LED TV 75″ ≈ €1,300, OLED 77″ ≈ €3,500.
