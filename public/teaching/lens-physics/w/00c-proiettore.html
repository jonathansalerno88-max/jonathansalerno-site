Simplified model. Matte screen: L = ρ · E / π, with E in lux (lx = lm/m²). In the dark the eye adapts and about 48 nits are enough, the DCI digital-cinema standard; with the lights on you need enough nits to keep the contrast at least 10:1 (the threshold chosen here for a watchable picture). Projector native contrast 1,000:1. The ALR screen is modelled with ρ 0.8 for the projector light and 0.15 for room light.
