The circulatory homunculus: systemic and pulmonary circulation and the two portal systems (liver: vein–capillary–vein; kidney: artery–capillary–artery), next to the patient in bed at 45°. Tapping a capillary bed shows the Starling equation along the capillary; with the albumin and central venous pressure sliders you see when the venous end also filters and oedema appears.
